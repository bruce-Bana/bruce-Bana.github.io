A little about me

I’m Bruce Bana, an independent software developer based in Kampala, Uganda.

Curiosity, then
craft.

I got into software by wanting to understand how technology works and how it can be made useful. That curiosity has grown into hands-on work across AI applications, developer tools, desktop software and Android.

I learn by building: starting with a problem, testing an approach, finding what breaks and improving the result. I’m especially interested in software that gives people more control over their tools and makes complex capabilities easier to use.

This site is a record of selected work and experiments. Projects are labelled by their current stage so that work in progress is not mistaken for a finished release.
